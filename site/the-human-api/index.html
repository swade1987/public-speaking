The Human API

Engineering Better Conversations

Talk description

You can architect a system that handles millions of requests per second, but freeze when your manager asks, "How's it going?"

Welcome to the engineering paradox: we're fluent in multiple programming languages, but struggle with the one that pays our salary - human connection.

In this high-energy, hands-on session, Steve (the engineer who once tried to small-talk about GitOps at a wedding) reveals the systematic approach he built to fix it.

Forget soft-skills fluff. This is communication for engineers, by an engineer who's debugged both platforms AND personalities across £100M+ in transformations.

Through live exercises and (deliberately awkward) roleplay, you'll practice the exact techniques that helped one engineer go from rejected ideas to VP of Engineering in six months.

Come ready to stand up and speak up. Warning: you might actually enjoy your next standup.

What You'll Learn

  • The Killer Question: makes any conversation easier to start, and got Steve three advisory board positions.
  • The Gesture Stack: three movements that command immediate authority in a room.
  • The Triple-P Method: Problem → Process → Payoff, for explaining Kubernetes to your CEO without their eyes glazing over.
  • Why you sound like a robot in meetings: and the 30-second fix that stops it.

Who It's For

Engineers, platform engineers, and tech leads who can architect a system but freeze in the room where its budget gets decided, or whose ideas get talked over instead of acted on.
