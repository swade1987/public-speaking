Speaker bio

I'm Steve Wade, The Pragmatic CNCF Guy and Founder of Platform Fix. Fifteen years in production infrastructure. Kubernetes since v1.2, back in 2015. Former Flux maintainer, Istio contributor, Kubernetes Ambassador for the DevOps Society.

I've watched over £100M disappear into complexity nobody asked for. Across 50+ platform rescues, the average audit recovers £276K a year.

Most consultants add complexity. I remove it. I built the Complexity Score to diagnose the damage, then the Deletion Protocol to remove it, in order, without adding a single new tool.

I've trained 6,000+ engineers across KubeCon, DevOps Days, DevOps Pro Europe, and enterprise engagements worldwide. Featured on the official Kubernetes Podcast and TGIK. I speak from experience, not theory.
